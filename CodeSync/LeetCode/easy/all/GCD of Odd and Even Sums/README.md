## Problem Description

You are given an integer n. Your task is to compute the GCD (greatest common divisor) of two values: sumOdd: the sum of the smallest n positive odd numbers. sumEven: the sum of the smallest n positive even numbers. Return the GCD of sumOdd and sumEven. Example 1: Input: n = 4 Output: 4 Explanation: Sum of the first 4 odd numbers sumOdd = 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 = 16 Sum of the first 4 even numbers sumEven = 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 = 20 Hence, GCD(sumOdd, sumEven) = GCD(16, 20) = 4. Example 2: Input: n = 5 Output: 5 Explanation: Sum of the first 5 odd numbers sumOdd = 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 25 Sum of the first 5 even numbers sumEven = 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10 = 30 Hence, GCD(sumOdd, sumEven) = GCD(25, 30) = 5. Constraints: 1 <= n <= 10​​​​​​​00
