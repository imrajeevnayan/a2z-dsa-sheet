## Problem Description

In the given range [L, R], print all numbers having unique digits. e.g. In range 10 to 20 should print all numbers except 11. Example 1: Input: L = 10 R = 20 Output: 10 12 13 14 15 16 17 18 19 20 Explanation: The number 11 has two 1 therefore 11 is not a unique number. Example 2: Input: L = 1 R = 9 Output: 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 Explanation: All the Numbers are unique. Your Task: You don't need to read input or print anything. Your task is to complete the function uniqueNumbers() which takes two integers L and R as an input parameter and returns the list/vector of all the unique numbers present between L to R. Expected Time Complexity: O(N) Expected Auxiliary Space: O(1) Constraints: 1 <= L <= R <= 104
