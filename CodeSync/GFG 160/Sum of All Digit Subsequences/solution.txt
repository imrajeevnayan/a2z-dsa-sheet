Input: s = "123" 
Output: 24
Explanation: The non-empty subsequences are: [1], [2], [3], [1, 2], [1, 3], [2, 3], [1, 2, 3]. The sum of all digits in these subsequences is: 1 + 2 + 3 + (1 + 2) + (1 + 3) + (2 + 3) + (1 + 2 + 3) = 24.
